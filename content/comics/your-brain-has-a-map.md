Your brain isn't one big blob — different regions specialise in different jobs.

## The four lobes

1. **Frontal lobe** — planning, decisions, personality, and the motor cortex that moves your body.
2. **Parietal lobe** — touch, body position and spatial awareness.
3. **Temporal lobe** — hearing, language comprehension and (with the hippocampus nearby) memory.
4. **Occipital lobe** — vision, right at the back.

## Below the lobes

- **Cerebellum** — balance and smooth, coordinated movement.
- **Brainstem** — the life-support crew: breathing, heart rate, sleep–wake.

> **Remember:** the regions specialise, but they constantly talk to each other through white matter.
