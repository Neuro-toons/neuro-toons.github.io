Lists are hard to remember. Stories aren't. So let's turn the cell into a **city**.

## The cell city

- **Nucleus** — city hall. Stores the blueprints (DNA) and decides what gets built.
- **Ribosomes** — the factories. They read instructions and build proteins.
- **Endoplasmic reticulum** — the assembly lines and roads. Rough ER (studded with ribosomes) makes proteins; smooth ER makes lipids.
- **Golgi apparatus** — the post office. Packages, labels and ships proteins.
- **Mitochondria** — the power plants. Make ATP, the cell's energy currency.
- **Lysosomes** — the recycling centre. Break down waste and worn-out parts.
- **Cell membrane** — the city border. Controls what comes in and goes out.

> **Mnemonic:** follow a protein's journey — *city hall → factory → assembly line → post office → out the border.*
