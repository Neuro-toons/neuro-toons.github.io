A gene can't be read from just anywhere. Transcription starts at a stretch of DNA called the **promoter** — the gene's start button.

## How it works

- The promoter sits just **upstream** of the gene.
- **Transcription factors** recognise and bind specific sequences in it.
- They recruit **RNA polymerase**, which starts copying the gene into RNA.

## Famous promoter sequences

- **Eukaryotes:** many promoters have a **TATA box**, roughly 25–35 bases upstream of the start site.
- **Bacteria:** the **−10 (Pribnow box)** and **−35** sequences position RNA polymerase.

> **Remember:** no promoter, no transcription — the gene stays silent.
