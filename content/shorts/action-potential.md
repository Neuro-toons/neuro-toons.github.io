An action potential is the electrical spike neurons use to send signals. It happens in four quick steps.

1. **Rest** — the inside of the neuron sits at about **−70 mV**.
2. **Depolarize** — a stimulus pushes it to threshold (about −55 mV). Voltage-gated **Na⁺ channels** open, Na⁺ rushes in, and the inside shoots up to around +30 mV.
3. **Repolarize** — Na⁺ channels inactivate and voltage-gated **K⁺ channels** open; K⁺ flows out and the voltage falls.
4. **Reset** — a brief undershoot (hyperpolarization), then back to rest. The **Na⁺/K⁺ pump** keeps the ion gradients in shape over time.

> **Exam tip:** it's *all-or-none* — reach threshold and you get a full spike; fall short and you get nothing.
